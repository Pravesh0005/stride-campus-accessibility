## 1. Executive Summary

During the field evaluation conducted across the 600-acre Lovely Professional University campus, the engineering team mapped 52 key structural facilities, cataloged 70+ pedestrian corridors, and evaluated accessibility parameters across 4 core vectors:
1. **Ramp Gradients & Plinth Transitions** (Standard: $\le 1:12$ slope, dual-height handrails at 750mm and 900mm).
2. **Vertical Transportation (Elevator Reliability)** (Clear door opening width $\ge 900\text{mm}$, tactile buttons, audible arrival annunciators).
3. **Threshold Clearances & Door Usability** (Zero threshold lips $\le 13\text{mm}$, automatic sliding or low opening force $\le 22\text{N}$).
4. **Sanitary Convenience** (Wheelchair accessible cubicles, inward swing clearance or outward swinging doors, grab bars).

The campus-wide average accessibility score was measured at **74%**, with 21 facilities rated **Certified Accessible ($\ge 80$)**, 29 facilities rated **Moderate Barriers (50–79)**, and 2 facilities identified as requiring **Urgent Remediation ($< 50$)**.

## 4. Key Recommendations for Campus Administration

1. **Pave the Agricultural Polyhouse Corridors (Block 27)**: Construct a 1.8-meter wide concrete pathway connecting Block 27 with Block 28 to eliminate unpaved dirt trails.
2. **Resurface Block 13 Heavy Workshop Entrances**: Install cast-iron threshold ramps over the 4-inch kerb to enable wheelchair students to reach manufacturing labs safely.
3. **Automate Fire Safety Double Doors in Block 25 & 34**: Fit magnetic door holders and handicap push-plates to eliminate heavy door drag forces.
4. **Deploy Stride QR Placards**: Affix Stride scan plates at all 52 building entrance portals so students can immediately view verified ramp directions and live elevator alerts.
